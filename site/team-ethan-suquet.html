Our people

Ethan Suquet

  • Member, Board of Trustees, United States
  • IT Coordinator

Ethan Suquet is CORAfrica’s IT Coordinator and a member of its United States Board of Trustees, which he joined in July 2025. He has served as the board’s Vice Chairman since September 2026.

A software developer by profession, he works for The Storyhaus Agency in Zelienople, Pennsylvania. For CORAfrica he builds and maintains the website and the systems behind it — the charity’s public face, and the way most of its supporters first find the work.

A devout Catholic, he is a member of Most Precious Blood of Jesus Parish and lives in Evans City, Pennsylvania. He was introduced to CORAfrica by Jeannine Goelz.

A registered 501(c)(3) non-profit in the United States and a registered NGO in Nigeria, working with rural communities from Cross River State to Abuja.
