₦150,000 started a business. $40,000 started a programme.

Ada Okoli took a ₦150,000 soft loan in 2022 and turned a small trade into a wholesale and retail business. The St. Thomas Aquinas empowerment programme launched with $40,000 and has since supported more than 500 women. Small sums, placed carefully, compound.
