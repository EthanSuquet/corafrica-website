Our schools

Built where there was nothing.

We found schools where the need is greatest — and then hand them over to be owned and run by the local diocese. The work is built to outlast whoever started it.

The register

Every school CORAfrica has founded or upgraded.

Some of these are now run by the Diocese of Ogoja. That is the intended end state, not a loss — a project that cannot be handed on has not really been built.
