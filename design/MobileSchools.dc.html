Our schools

Built where there was nothing.

We found schools where the need is greatest — then hand them over to be owned and run by the local diocese.

The register

Every school CORAfrica has founded or upgraded.

Some are now run by the Diocese of Ogoja. That is the intended end state — a project that cannot be handed on has not really been built.

John Stilley Secondary School
300+ students Victoria, Ikom 2021
Founded where the community had no secondary school at all. A new classroom block has since been added.
John Bosco Academy
Refugee response Adagom, Ogoja 2020
Founded to reach refugee children arriving from Cameroon who could not access basic education.
St. Peter’s Primary School
300+ pupils Adagom 3, Ogoja 2021
A six-classroom block serving children from refugee and host communities who cannot afford fees.
St. Joseph’s Primary & Secondary
2,000+ pupils Idum-Mbube —
Also an orphanage. Now handed over to be owned and run by the Diocese of Ogoja.
Little Flower Nursery & Primary
Nursery & primary Ipong-Obudu —
One of the earliest schools initiated across the diocese.
Thomas McGettrick Institute
Technical Ogoja Diocese —
A technical institute facilitated with the diocesan bishops, and since handed on.
