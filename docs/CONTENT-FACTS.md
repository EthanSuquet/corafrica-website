## The CEC model — resolved
The docx's **"What We Do"** lists three things (Education, VASAC, Healthcare), but the docx's own
**"Our Model"** section describes **four**: education, health, agriculture, and economic
empowerment. Vanguard, one month old, states the CEC model is those same **four** components.
**Conclusion: the model is four pillars.** The docx's "What We Do" is simply under-written, not a
strategic change. Building on four.

Two CECs exist, in **Mbube-Ogoja** and **Victoria-Ikom** (live site).

### ⚠️ Security framing — a judgment call, not a fact to copy across
S9 (to camera) names "attacks from armed Fulani herdsmen and other militant groups"; S7 refers to
"Christian communities facing threats from violent groups, including Fulani herdsmen in some areas."
These are Fr. Peter's own words and the fear is real. But naming an ethnic group as the aggressor on
a public website is a different act from saying it on a video call: it can read badly to
international NGO funders and it can raise risk for staff on the ground. **The site currently
describes displacement and insecurity factually — the Cameroonian refugee influx, which is
well-documented and neutral — and leaves the naming of armed groups to Fr. Peter.** Flagged for his
decision; the omission is deliberate, not an oversight.
