## The CEC model — resolved
The docx's **"What We Do"** lists three things (Education, VASAC, Healthcare), but the docx's own
**"Our Model"** section describes **four**: education, health, agriculture, and economic
empowerment. Vanguard, one month old, states the CEC model is those same **four** components.
**Conclusion: the model is four pillars.** The docx's "What We Do" is simply under-written, not a
strategic change. Building on four.

Two CECs exist, in **Mbube-Ogoja** and **Victoria-Ikom** (live site).
