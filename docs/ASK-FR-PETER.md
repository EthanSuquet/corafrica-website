replacements.

21. **The Help-a-Kid programme.** NCR describes it (scholarships, educational materials, school

## Added after reading the four documents supplied 2026-09-01

The 2025 financial report, the Adagom building proposal, the Mission Showcase one-pager and the
SENT video script. Between them they closed items 7 and 10 (partly), answered item 9, and raised
these.

30. 🔴 **Confirm John Bosco University should be public.** It is now the lead section of the
    Strategic Plan page, at approximately **$2 million**, because his own one-pager calls it "our
    primary priority" and the video script "our biggest and most urgent dream". But it appears
    **nowhere in the docx and nowhere on the current site**, which is a strange omission for a
    stated top priority. Either it is newer than the docx, or it is not meant to be public yet.
    Ask before launch.
31. **Current status of the Adagom academic building.** The proposal timelines Q1 2026 planning,
    **Q2–Q3 2026 construction**, Q4 2026 first cohort. It is now September 2026 — so where does it
    actually stand, and how much of the $129,158.62 is still needed? The page describes it as
    "under construction". A funder will ask, and a stale number is worse than no number.
32. **A ₦29,000,000 arithmetic error in the Adagom proposal.** The line-item table foots to
    **₦216,280,000**, but the four components sum to **₦187,280,000**, which is also what the
    "budget at a glance" table and the $129,158.62 figure require. The site uses the dollar
    figure, which is consistent — but **the proposal document itself should be corrected before it
    goes to any more funders.** Worth flagging to him and to Mark Okpatuma quietly.
33. **The proposal disagrees with itself on outcomes.** Executive summary: ~100 beneficiaries a
    year at a **60% enterprise launch rate**. Objectives: at least 100 at **70% self-employment**.
    Economic analysis: **200 trainees a year**. Enrolment: **479** in one section, **"498–500"** in
    another. None of these are on the site — we used only the audited 479 — but pick one set
    before the proposal circulates further.
34. ⚠️ **How to describe the security situation.** The video script names "attacks from armed
    Fulani herdsmen and other militant groups"; the proposal refers to threats to Christian
    communities from "violent groups, including Fulani herdsmen". **The site does not repeat
    this** — it describes the Cameroonian refugee influx and displacement factually instead. That
    omission is deliberate: naming an ethnic group as aggressor on a public site reads differently

    staff on the ground. **This is his call, not ours** — ask him explicitly which framing he wants.
35. **Is "John Bosco Academy (St Peter)" one school or two?** *(Sharpens the old question at the
    bottom of the list.)* The audited accounts book the education spend under that single heading,

    section (136). That strongly suggests **St. Peter's Primary is the academy's primary section**,
    not a separate school — but the Schools page still lists them as two rows, which risks
    double-counting pupils. Confirm and we will merge them.
36. **Is the Adagom building's science and computer equipment already bought?** The "What it buys"
    prices on the Donate page ($34 a desk, $172 a computer, $13,269 a complete science lab) come

    `[REQUEST FROM FR. PETER]` in every page footer. *(Same as item 6 — restated because it is now
    the only registration number missing.)*
38. **Would he like the Franciscan University of Steubenville partnership named on the site?** The
