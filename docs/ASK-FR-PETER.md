## 🟠 Needed to make the site win grants
_This is the material that separates a nice-looking site from one a grant officer takes seriously._

6. **The 501(c)(3) EIN and a scan of the IRS determination letter.** Press confirms the status was
   granted in 2006, but the number itself is nowhere public. Grant portals ask for it first.
7. **The Nigerian CAC registration number** (and the registered entity name). Vanguard says
   CORAfrica is registered with the relevant agencies but publishes no number.
8. **Current impact numbers**, even approximate, with an as-of date:
   - total children currently enrolled across all schools
   - number of schools and clinics operating **today**
   - number of communities served
   - number of staff and teachers employed
   - women supported through the empowerment programmes to date
   _The only figures we have are 2,000+ pupils and 500+ women, both from a 2024 NCR article. They
   are almost certainly out of date and understate the work._
9. **A one-page financial summary** — annual budget, and a rough "where the money goes" split.
   Even three numbers is a major trust signal, and almost no peer NGO site has it.
10. **Named projects with funding targets and scope** — the Children's Hospital Project, a VASAC
    build, and a Community Education Center. Cost, timeline, what it delivers, how many children.
    This is the page a grant officer actually clicks.
11. **The Strategic Plan document.** The docx section says "To be continued" and lists only three
    programme names. If the real plan exists as a document, we can publish a summary and link a PDF.
12. **Confirmation of the SDG alignment** Vanguard reported (poverty, food security, gender
    equality, decent work, reduced inequality). Grant-makers screen on these — it deserves its own
    block, and it is currently on neither the docx nor the site.

## Added after building the eight sub-pages (2026-09-01)

Building the full site surfaced a few more gaps. These are additions to the list above, not
replacements.

21. **The Help-a-Kid programme.** NCR describes it (scholarships, educational materials, school
    uniforms) but the docx never mentions it. Is it still running, and how many children are on
    it now? It is the single easiest thing for a small donor to give to, and it currently has no
    page of its own.
22. **Confirm the hand-over framing.** The Schools page states that St. Joseph's has been handed
    to the Diocese of Ogoja, and presents that as the *intended end state* — proof the model
    works, not a project lost. That reads well to a grant officer, but it is our framing, not
    his. Check he is comfortable with it.
23. **Which projects are still CORAfrica's, and which have been handed on?** The docx says "all
    these initiatives are eventually handed over to be owned and run by his home diocese." The
    site currently needs to say clearly which of the schools, clinics and programmes CORAfrica
    still runs today, because a funder will ask exactly that.
24. **The two Community Education Centres.** The live site said both Mbube-Ogoja and
    Victoria-Ikom are awaiting funding to be "fully equipped and operated". Is that still true,
    and what would fully equipping one actually cost? That is a fundable, concrete ask and there
    is currently no number attached to it.
25. **The Sr. Augustina Abuo Medical Clinic** — is the spelling *Abuo* or *Abue*? The docx uses
    both spellings in nearby sentences.
26. **A photo of the Board and the Administrative Team**, once the lists are confirmed — the
    Contact page has cards for them and no faces.
